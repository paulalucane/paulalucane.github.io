Pink Tax Research Report

This research report explores a contemporary issue - the so called "pink tax". This specific issue spoke to me personally, as I am regularly affected by it, as are millions of women around the world who experience the same kind of inequality based on their gender alone. I researched several aspects related to pink tax, such as the gender pay gap, and created my own visual materials, including graphics that showcase statistics and illustrations that represent the nature of the issue. Additionally, this report considers the issue through the lens of graphic design - its influence and how it could be used to address the issue in the future.

Created with: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign
